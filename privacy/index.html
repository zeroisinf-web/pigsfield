Privacy & terms

Use the platform with clear boundaries.

Pigsfield does not require a visitor account. Some features store preferences on your device or contact a clearly identified third-party service when you choose to use them.

Information stored on your device

Theme and language preferences, the selected AI model and a random service rate-limit identifier use browser local storage. Pigsfield does not keep a history of the resources you open. The identifier helps apply short per-visitor AI and translation limits; it is not a visitor account or profile. These values do not sync between devices, and clearing site data removes them.

The homepage visitor counter uses two first-party, HTTP-only cookies: one holds only the current India calendar day and expires after two days, so a browser is counted as a visit once a day; the other holds only the value 1 and lasts up to 400 days, so a browser is counted as a person once. A browser is counted only after someone scrolls, taps or types on the homepage, and never when it is recognisably a bot, crawler or cloud server. The counter stores totals, per-day visit counts for the last 45 days and the date counting began—not visitor identities, raw addresses or profiles. Cookies can be blocked or cleared and one person may use multiple browsers, so the displayed numbers are explicitly best-effort browser check-in counts rather than an exact count of people.

No sign-in

Pigsfield has no accounts and nothing on the site is behind one. The earlier optional sign-in, which only synced a list saved with the heart button, has been retired along with that button; resources are now shared as ordinary links instead. Sharing opens your own device's share sheet, WhatsApp, Telegram or email with a link to the page, and Pigsfield records nothing about it. If you used the old sign-in, email zeroisinf@gmail.com to have that account and its saved list deleted.

Hindi translation

Choosing हिन्दी translates loaded translatable interface text in place and remembers that language preference on this device. Where the browser supports its Translator API, translation uses a browser-provided on-device model. Otherwise, loaded translatable English interface text—including page copy, accessible labels, titles and placeholders—is sent in limited batches only to Pigsfield's same-origin /api/translate endpoint, which uses Cloudflare-hosted AI4Bharat IndicTrans2. Text typed by the visitor is never included. The request is rate-limited and uses a no-referrer policy. It does not include the page URL, require a visitor account or provider key, or send text to Google Translate. The original English text is retained so EN can restore it instantly. Browser-menu guidance appears only if both translation paths fail; a browser vendor's terms apply if a visitor then chooses that browser feature. The English page remains the source version.

YouTube playback

The player is created only after you press Play and uses YouTube's privacy-enhanced domain. YouTube still receives the network information necessary to deliver the player. The site preserves a strict-origin referrer because YouTube requires site identity for embedded playback. If you do not want that connection, use the original link or do not start the player.

Cover art

PigBang cards show real cover art for each title, channel or app. Your browser requests it from Pigsfield's own /api/poster address, never from Netflix, Prime Video, Hotstar, YouTube, Google Play or the App Store. Pigsfield reads the same public link-preview image those providers publish for shared links, caches it at the edge, and serves it back from this site. The provider sees a request from Pigsfield, not from you, and learns nothing about which card you looked at. Where a provider publishes no usable image, the card keeps its generated symbol.

AI studio

The text selector offers gemma-4-26b-a4b-it (@cf/google/gemma-4-26b-a4b-it), gpt-oss-120b (@cf/openai/gpt-oss-120b) and llama-4-scout-17b-16e-instruct (@cf/meta/llama-4-scout-17b-16e-instruct), all hosted through Cloudflare Workers AI. Tutor and document prompts are sent to Pigsfield's same-origin /api/ai endpoint. Visitors do not log in, provide an additional provider key or download a model. Pigsfield and Cloudflare receive the request information needed to process a prompt. Rate limits, shared allocation and service availability apply.

Image prompts use the named Pollinations image service, so those prompts and the network information needed for the request reach Pollinations. Voice preview and music synthesis are offered only when the browser exposes the necessary capability and their output is made in the browser. Generated images, documents and music files remain downloadable where the browser supports the format. External links to Artificial Analysis and Qwen Chat open those providers' own sites and terms. Do not submit personal, confidential, copyrighted or sensitive information to any cloud service. Generated output may be inaccurate, biased or unsuitable and must be reviewed.

External links and ownership

Acceptable use and limitations

Use Pigsfield lawfully and do not use the AI studio to create abuse, impersonation, harassment, sexual content involving minors, fraud or infringement. Education, exam and civic information is general guidance, not a professional guarantee or legal opinion.

Resources may change or contain errors. Report material problems promptly. Contact: zeroisinf@gmail.com.
