Open doors, not barriers
Good resources are everywhere. Let’s make them easier to reach.

Useful knowledge is scattered

Government portals, universities, channels, apps and public-interest organizations each hold part of the answer. Pigsfield brings the paths together without copying ownership.

Cost and search overload exclude people

Learners spend time comparing level, price, language and relevance before they can even begin. Free-first organization reduces that friction.

Education must connect to opportunity

Learning is stronger when it leads to practical skills, better tools, exam confidence, livelihood options and the ability to navigate public systems.

Agency is part of social justice

A person’s income, language, location or social background should not decide whether they can learn, build a livelihood or seek public accountability.

Questions

A few things worth knowing.

Is every resource free?

Pigsfield is free to browse and prioritizes free and public options. Some third-party films, apps or courses are paid or may require an account; those labels come from the catalog and should be checked at the original source.

Does Pigsfield own these courses and videos?

No. Pigsfield is a discovery and guidance layer. Copyright and availability remain with each original provider.

Can I save or open a resource in a new tab?

Yes. Every provider control is a real link: right-click it on desktop or press and hold it on mobile for the browser's normal new-tab and copy-link actions. Your device-local saved list stays in this browser unless you clear its storage.

How can I correct a broken or unsafe link?

The feedback button is fixed on every page. Send the title, page and correct source; community corrections are central to keeping Pigsfield useful.

The mission

Build the alternative you wish everyone had.

Pigsfield is a volunteer-led attempt to make high-quality learning easier to reach, especially for people who have limited money, language options or institutional guidance.
