Questions

A few things worth knowing.

Is every resource free?

Browsing Pigsfield is free and prioritizes open public options. A few linked third-party apps or courses are paid; check each card label at the source.

Does Pigsfield own these courses and videos?

No. Copyright and availability stay with each original provider.

Can I share a resource or open it in a new tab?

Yes—use any card's share icon for WhatsApp, Telegram, or email, and open any source link directly.

How can I correct a broken or unsafe link?

Tap Feedback in the bottom corner or email us via Submit; community reports keep links reliable.
