The problem we solve

Excellent educational and public-interest resources already exist, but they are scattered across hundreds of government portals, universities, NGOs, YouTube channels, apps and commercial platforms. People lose time finding them, comparing cost, checking relevance and working out what to do next.

That friction is not neutral. It weighs most heavily on people with limited money, bandwidth, language options, social networks or institutional guidance. When education is treated only as a transaction, capable learners are filtered out before their curiosity has a chance.

How Pigsfield helps

Access

Prioritize free-first, public and open options while clearly acknowledging when a source is paid or unlabelled.

Direction

Organize resources by stage and goal so a learner can identify a useful next step.

Transparency

Link to original providers, explain the limits of the catalog and make corrections easy to submit.

Opportunity

Connect formal learning with teaching, skills, tools, exams, employment and creative capability.

Agency

Include citizen-rights and accountability channels because public participation is part of dignity.

Community

Invite users to report problems, suggest stronger sources and volunteer their expertise.

Who benefits

  • Parents and learners from early childhood through Class 12.
  • Undergraduate, postgraduate and PhD learners.
  • Teachers, school leaders and teacher-exam candidates.
  • Job seekers, vocational learners and people building employable skills.
  • UPSC, RAS, SSC, banking and other competitive-exam aspirants.
  • Citizens seeking RTI, grievance, legal-aid and accountability channels.
  • Volunteers and donors who want to strengthen public access to knowledge.

The organization stays coherent

Pigsfield keeps six destinations in one consistent order: Nursery to PhD; PigBang; Competitive Exams; Vocational & Business; Digital Tools; and Make Govt Accountable. Teacher Training is highlighted directly below PhD because strong teaching helps every stage of education flourish. The always-available AI Studio supports these destinations instead of becoming another one to navigate.

This structure follows a person's journey: learn, understand the world through media, prepare for opportunity, build practical capability, use better tools, and participate in public life.

What we do not claim

Pigsfield does not own or endorse every linked resource, guarantee that every third-party source is free, promise exam results, provide legal advice, or claim formal government recognition. Availability, price and policies can change. Strong trust is built through visible corrections—not through “world's best” slogans.

The ambition is large: become one of the most useful educational discovery platforms in the world. That status has to be earned through usefulness, accessibility and editorial discipline.
