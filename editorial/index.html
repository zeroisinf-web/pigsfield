Trust & editorial policy

Useful curation must be transparent and correctable.

Pigsfield is a discovery layer, not the owner of linked content. These principles explain what belongs, what does not, and how the catalog should improve.

Source prioritySelection criteriaLabelsSafetyCorrections

Source priority

Prefer the original provider and, where relevant, official government, public university, recognized institutional, open educational or creator-owned sources. Avoid copied files, link shorteners, piracy, credential theft, deceptive downloads and pages that obscure ownership.

Selection criteria

  • Clear educational, skill, exam or citizen value.
  • A direct and reasonably stable source.
  • Audience and purpose that can be explained without hype.
  • Free or public options prioritized; paid options included only when their role is clear.
  • No fabricated ratings, endorsements, guarantees or impact claims.

Cost and access labels

“Free” means the catalog currently identifies a no-payment route; “Paid” means payment is expected; and “Check source” means the data is incomplete or availability may vary. Provider pricing, login requirements and regional access can change, so the original source is authoritative.

The catalog is being upgraded toward stronger fields such as provider, language, cost, account requirement, license and last checked date. Missing labels must not be presented as verified facts.

Special safeguards

Civic information

Use official sources first, state jurisdiction clearly and treat practical notes as general education—not legal advice.

Media

Do not link to piracy or evade a creator's embedding decision. When YouTube blocks embedding, link to the original page.

AI

State the provider, privacy boundary, rate limits and experimental nature. Never expose an owner API key in browser code.

Corrections and removal

Report a dead, unsafe, misleading or improperly attributed resource through the feedback button. Include the page, title, problem and better source. Urgent safety or rights concerns should be identified in the email subject.
