Jobs, Business & Trades

Government job, apprenticeship, business and trade portals

Find work, start a business, learn a trade. Official portals for finding work and apprenticeships, registering a small business, applying for Mudra and PMEGP loans, and learning a trade through ITI or PM Vishwakarma.

9 free-first resources, each opening its original provider.

💼

National Career Service (NCS)

Government and private jobs, job fairs and career counselling. Free registration.

Webncs.gov.in
🌐

Apprenticeship India (NAPS)

Paid apprenticeships for 10th, 12th and ITI pass candidates.

Webapprenticeshipindia.gov.in
🌐

NATS (Graduate/Diploma Apprenticeship)

Paid apprenticeships for graduates and diploma holders.

Webnats.education.gov.in
🌐

e-Shram (ई-श्रम)

Free registration for unorganised workers, linked to social-security schemes.

Webeshram.gov.in
🏛️

Udyam Registration (उद्यम पंजीकरण)

Free online MSME registration with Aadhaar.

Webudyamregistration.gov.in
🎨

PM Mudra Yojana / JanSamarth

Collateral-free loans for small businesses: Shishu, Kishore and Tarun.

Webjansamarth.inmudra.org.in
🌐

PMEGP

Subsidy on bank loans for starting a new micro enterprise.

Webkviconline.gov.in
🌐

PM Vishwakarma (पीएम विश्वकर्मा)

Training, toolkit support and low-cost loans for traditional artisans.

Webdisclaimer.pmvishwakarma.gov.in
💼

Bharat Skills (DGT / ITI)

ITI trade syllabi, e-books, question banks and videos. Free.

Webbharatskills.gov.in
