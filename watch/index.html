Educational media

Watch with purpose, not an endless feed.

PigBang organizes educational films, shows, channels, playlists and apps by level and subject—then loads a player only when you choose to watch.

Playback that respects context:Direct YouTube videos and playlists open in one privacy-enhanced player. If an owner disables embedding, a video is removed, or a privacy tool blocks YouTube’s required referrer, the original-source button remains available.
