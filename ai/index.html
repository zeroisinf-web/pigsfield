It starts before you type

Opening the panel sends the page’s title, headings and visible text—nothing else—and it answers with three or four things worth doing on this page. Tap one, or ignore them and ask your own question.

A video becomes notes

When a video is playing, Ask AI is given the video itself and writes complete revision notes from it: sections, definitions, worked examples and a list of key points. Save them through your browser’s print dialogue as a PDF. If the video cannot be read, the notes say so rather than pretending.

Talk instead of typing

Speak your question and have the answer read back, using the speech your browser already provides. Nothing extra is installed, and the microphone only listens while you hold the conversation open.

Ask AI is answered by Google’s Gemini through the same-origin /api/ask endpoint, so the key stays on the server and never reaches your browser. The page text is sent as reference material, never as instructions, and nothing is stored between sessions. AI Studio, beside it, is now a comparison: the ten most intelligent models that answer end to end within 35 seconds—at most two from any one company—with cost per task and benchmark response time, synced from Artificial Analysis, plus the chat sites worth opening.
