Accessibility

Access is a product requirement, not a footer promise.

Pigsfield is designed to work across keyboard, touch, small screens, assistive technology and varied network conditions. The work is ongoing, and barrier reports are welcome.

Included now

  • Semantic headings, landmarks, breadcrumbs and native disclosure controls.
  • Keyboard-visible focus states and a skip-to-content link.
  • Text that reflows on small screens without desktop-window interactions.
  • Light and dark themes with system-aware defaults.
  • Reduced-motion support when the operating system requests it.
  • Search, device-local saving and genuine resource links with accessible labels.
  • Content remains readable when browser text is enlarged.

Video and generated media

YouTube caption availability belongs to each source. The Pigsfield player preserves YouTube controls and always offers the original link. AI-generated images, audio and short videos are experimental and may not include complete descriptions or transcripts unless the user creates them.

Known limitations

Some inherited resource descriptions are Hindi-only or English-only. In-page Hindi translation uses an on-device browser model where supported; otherwise, loaded translatable English interface text—including page copy, accessible labels, titles and placeholders—is sent in rate-limited batches to Pigsfield's same-origin endpoint and Cloudflare-hosted AI4Bharat. Text typed by the visitor is never included. No account, page URL or Google Translate request is involved. Browser-menu guidance appears only when both paths fail. Translation quality can vary. Third-party websites have their own accessibility quality. Large catalogs are generated with JavaScript, so a fully script-free resource listing is not yet available. These are tracked as product limitations, not hidden as exceptions.
