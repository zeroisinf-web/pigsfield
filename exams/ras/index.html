RAS

RAS syllabus with free courses, marathons and books for Prelims and Mains

The Rajasthan Administrative Services exam, conducted by the Rajasthan Public Service Commission as the RAS/RTS Combined Competitive Examination, has a single objective Prelims paper, four descriptive Mains papers and an interview.

Rajasthan's history, art and culture, geography and economy carry real weight at every stage, alongside the national General Studies syllabus it shares with UPSC. This page sets out the syllabus paper by paper, with a free course, a revision marathon and books under each topic. A practical order is NCERT books first, then Rajasthan-specific material, then previous papers to see how questions are actually framed.

All links open their original provider. Check the current notification on rpsc.rajasthan.gov.in, because the pattern and syllabus can change.

Prelims: Single Paper (MCQ)

GK & General Science Questions: 150|Marks: 200 1/3 -ve marking | Duration: 3 hours

A: History, Art, Culture, Literature, Tradition & Heritage of Rajasthan

Pre-historical sites; sources of Rajasthan history; ancient Rajasthan; major dynasties — Guhila, Pratihar, Chauhan, Parmar, Rathore, Sisodiya, Kachchawa; administrative and revenue system; 18th–19th century Rajasthan; peasant and tribal movements; Praja Mandal movements; integration of Rajasthan; temples, forts, palaces, monuments, water bodies; painting schools; handicrafts; folk dance, drama, music, instruments; Rajasthani dialects and literature; saints, sects, folk deities; fairs, festivals, customs, attire, ornaments. (Connect Civils)

Official syllabus · rajras.in

B: Indian History

Indus and Vedic Age; 6th century BC religious ideas — Ajivakas, Buddhism, Jainism; Maurya, Kushan, Satavahana, Gupta, Chalukya, Pallava, Chola; ancient art, architecture and science; Varna Ashram, Purushartha, Sanskara, philosophy and education; Sultanate; Vijayanagara; Mughal administration; Marathas; Bhakti and Sufi movements; British imperialism; Revolt of 1857; Indian National Movement; post-independence nation building up to 2000. (Connect Civils)

Official syllabus · rajras.in

C: Geography of World and India

World physical geography: mountains, plateaus, plains, deserts; major rivers and lakes; natural vegetation; agriculture; industries; transport; environmental issues — desertification, deforestation, climate change, global warming, ozone depletion. India: physiographic divisions, climate, rivers, lakes, irrigation, agriculture, minerals, industries. (Connect Civils)

Official syllabus · rajras.in

D: Geography of Rajasthan

Location, extent, physiography, rivers, lakes, climate, natural vegetation, biodiversity, conservation, soil, agriculture, livestock, irrigation, population, urbanisation, tribes, metallic and non-metallic minerals, tourism. (Connect Civils)

Official syllabus · rajras.in

E: Indian Constitution, Political System & Governance

Framing of Constitution, Preamble, Citizenship, Fundamental Rights, DPSP, Fundamental Duties, Union Executive, Legislature, Judiciary, Union-State relations, Emergency provisions, local government, Election Commission, UPSC, NHRC, NCW, NCPCR, NITI Aayog, public policy, citizen charter, social audit, grievance redressal, Lokpal, CVC, CIC. (Connect Civils)

Official syllabus · rajras.in

F: Political and Administrative System of Rajasthan

Governor, Chief Minister, Council of Ministers, Rajasthan Legislative Assembly, Rajasthan High Court, subordinate courts, Advocate General, Chief Secretary, State Secretariat, directorates, Divisional Commissioner, District Collector / District Magistrate, Superintendent of Police, SDO, Tehsildar, RPSC, State Election Commission, State Information Commission, State Women Commission, Board of Revenue, Lokayukt, Panchayati Raj and Municipal administration. (Connect Civils)

Official syllabus · rajras.in

G: Economic Concepts and Indian Economy

Economic growth and development; environmental degradation; sustainable development; HDI; monetary policy; fiscal policy; Union Budget; resource mobilisation; fiscal federalism; Finance Commission; agriculture; industrial growth; LPG reforms; service sector; energy; transport; communication; skill development; employment; social justice and empowerment. (Connect Civils)

Official syllabus · rajras.in

H: Economy of Rajasthan

Macro overview of Rajasthan economy; State Budget; agriculture, industry and service sector; infrastructure — energy, transport, communication; rural development; Panchayati Raj; State Finance Commission; education and health; welfare schemes of Rajasthan government. (Connect Civils)

Official syllabus · rajras.in

I: Science & Technology

Everyday science; computers; ICT; defence and space technology; inheritance and variation; genetic engineering; biotechnology; nanotechnology; human health; food and nutrition; diseases; public health programmes; ecology; biodiversity; conservation; sustainable development; agriculture, horticulture, forestry and animal husbandry with special reference to Rajasthan; government science policies; recent scientific and technological advancements. (Connect Civils)

Official syllabus · rajras.in

J: Reasoning & Mental Ability

Statement-assumption, statement-argument, statement-conclusion, course of action, analytical reasoning, number/letter sequence, coding-decoding, relations, direction sense, Venn diagram, ranking, sitting arrangement, shapes, ratio, proportion, partnership, profit-loss, percentage, SI-CI, perimeter, area, data analysis, mean, median, mode, permutation-combination, probability. (Connect Civils)
