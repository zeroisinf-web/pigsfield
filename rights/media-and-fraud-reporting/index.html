Media & Fraud Reporting

Complain to the Press Council or NBDSA, and report Aadhaar or DBT fraud

When the story or the money is wrong. Holding coverage to account, and reporting benefit fraud. Media complaints have short deadlines, so act while the broadcast is recent.

Local journalists को DM करो | 6. YouTube पर video documentary बनाओ | 7. Facebook groups में share करो | 8. Important: हमेशा factual रहो, defamation से बचो

सुझाव

Social Media को Strategic Weapon बनाओ: • Tag करो: @PMOIndia @rajCMO @Ministry + DM का handle + CM Office • कई IAS/IPS social media पर active हैं — Public Shaming से बचने को तुरंत act करते हैं • Photo + Time + Location + Officer का नाम = Maximum impact post • RTI reply की photo upload करो — यह irrefutable evidence है • Important: हमेशा factual रहो — defamation case से बचो • Data-driven posts (CAG report citing, affidavit data) = ज़्यादा credibility
